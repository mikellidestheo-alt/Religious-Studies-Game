# Flagged topics

Topics where Catholic teaching is not clear-cut, is disputed within Catholicism, or could easily be misrepresented in a four-option question. Dilemmas on these topics should be rewritten so one answer is clearly best, or left out. Add to this list at each stage.

## Stage 1

No Stage 1 sample dilemmas were dropped. Two areas to watch in later stages:

- **Remarriage after divorce and receiving Communion (2.6).** Since *Amoris Laetitia* (2016), bishops have applied the teaching on divorced-and-remarried Catholics receiving Communion in different ways. The game avoids this question. It tests only the clear points: civil divorce, annulment, and Communion for Catholics who are divorced but have **not** remarried.
- **Capital punishment (3.6).** The Catholic position changed in 2018 (CCC 2267). Older textbooks may still give the earlier teaching ("only in very rare cases"). The game treats the current Catechism as correct and uses the older position as a "partly correct" wrong option, explaining the change in the feedback.

## Possible future flags (Stages 2+)

- **Predestination (P1 1.4).** Catholic teaching rejects double predestination, but grace, free will and God's foreknowledge are subtle. Questions will test only the clear contrast with Calvinist predestination.
- **Just War today (P1 3.1).** Recent popes have questioned whether modern warfare can meet the Just War conditions. Questions will test the traditional criteria, without claiming a fixed verdict on any modern war.
- **Evolution and creation (P1 1.1).** Catholic teaching accepts that evolution can be compatible with faith, but there is no single official view on the details. Questions will avoid the details.
- **Genetic engineering (P1 2.8).** The Church distinguishes therapy from enhancement and from methods that destroy embryos. Questions will stick to these clear distinctions.
