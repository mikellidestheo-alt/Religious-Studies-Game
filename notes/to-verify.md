# To verify

Things a teacher or subject specialist should check before students rely on them. Add to this list at each stage. Tick items off when checked.

## General note on sources

The Edexcel 4RS1 specification's indicative content describes Christianity in general terms. It does not set out a specifically Catholic position on each topic. The "correct" answers in this game come from the **Catechism of the Catholic Church (CCC)** and standard Catholic teaching, so they draw on general knowledge beyond the specification. Wrong options that are labelled as Protestant, Anglican, Humanist and so on are broad, simplified summaries. Views differ inside every one of those groups.

Scripture references give the book, chapter and verse only. Wording in feedback paraphrases the passage; it does not quote any translation, except for the very short phrases noted below.

## Stage 1 (12 sample dilemmas)

- [ ] **CCC paragraph numbers** in `sources` fields: CCC 80-82, 309-314, 538-540, 1182, 1250-1252, 1261, 1383, 1438, 1577, 1629, 1650, 1776-1785, 2266, 2267, 2276-2279, 2334, 2402-2406, 2415-2418. These are given from memory of the CCC's structure. Each should be checked against the official text (vatican.va).
- [ ] **p1s3-capital-01**: CCC 2267 was revised in 2018 under Pope Francis to call the death penalty "inadmissible". Please confirm the date and wording. The feedback says "many atheists and Humanists also oppose capital punishment". I believe Humanists UK opposes the death penalty, but this needs checking.
- [ ] **p1s2-annulment-01**: the wrong-option note says the Church of England allows remarriage after divorce in some cases, at the priest's discretion (I understand this has been so since 2002). Also: "divorced Catholics who have not remarried can receive Communion". This is standard teaching, but please check the wording suits your school.
- [ ] **p1s2-euthanasia-01**: the teaching that refusing "burdensome" treatment and giving pain relief that may shorten life are permitted (CCC 2278-2279). Check that the simplified wording is acceptable.
- [ ] **p2s2-lent-01**: "the strict fasting rules are for adults". Under general canon law, fasting binds ages 18-59 and abstinence from meat from age 14. Rules differ between bishops' conferences (for example, Friday abstinence in England and Wales). The feedback keeps this deliberately general.
- [ ] **p1s4-wealth-01**: the phrase "the love of money" (1 Timothy 6:10) is a short phrase common to most English translations. The camel saying in the scene is the penitent's own words, not a quotation from a set translation. The label "prosperity gospel" is a simplification.
- [ ] **p1s4-women-01**: "several women saints are honoured as great teachers of the faith" refers to the women Doctors of the Church (for example, St Teresa of Avila and St Catherine of Siena). The wrong-option note says the Church of England and the Methodist Church ordain women.
- [ ] **p1s1-stewardship-01**: Laudato Si' (2015), Pope Francis. Its description of the Earth as "our common home" is paraphrased, not quoted.
- [ ] **p2s1-conscience-01**: "many Baptists" hold sola scriptura. This is a broad generalisation.
- [ ] **p2s3-altar-01**: the claims that "many Protestant churches put the pulpit at the centre" and that Quaker meeting houses are plain are both generalisations.

## Stage 2: Paper 1 Section 1

- [ ] **p1s1-creation-science-01**: Fr Georges Lemaître, a Belgian Catholic priest, first proposed what became the Big Bang theory (1927). I am confident of this, but it should be checked before it is taught. Also check CCC 159 and 282-289.
- [ ] **p1s1-reincarnation-01**: the wrong-option note says 'soul sleep' is held by Seventh-day Adventists. Check CCC 1013 and 1021-1022 for one earthly life and immediate judgment.
- [ ] **p1s1-trinity-01**: the wrong-option note says the Arian view is close to Jehovah's Witnesses' beliefs. This is a simplification, and should be worded sensitively.
- [ ] **p1s1-predestination-01**: the wrong-option note says double predestination is "close to the view of John Calvin, held in some Reformed churches". This is a simplification. 'Open theism' is labelled as a minority Protestant view.
- [ ] **p1s1-ignorance-01**: CCC 1857-1860 (conditions for mortal sin; ignorance can reduce responsibility). Check that the paragraph numbers match.
- [ ] **p1s1-doubt-01**: CCC 2088 (voluntary and involuntary doubt) and CCC 162 (perseverance in faith).
- [ ] **p1s1-unbelief-01**: CCC 847 (those who, through no fault of their own, do not know Christ). The wording is a simplification.
- [ ] **p1s1-freewill-fate-01**: Sirach 15:14-15 (God left humans in the power of their own free choice). This book is in Catholic Bibles but not in most Protestant ones.
- [ ] Other references used: Genesis 1:27; 2:7; Luke 12:15; Luke 13:1-5; Luke 15:11-32; Romans 3:23; 1 Timothy 2:4; Psalm 139:1-4; Matthew 10:29-31; Matthew 28:19; Mark 9:24; Hebrews 9:27; Exodus 20:15; 1 Corinthians 6:19-20. The game gives these references only. The short phrase "temple of the Holy Spirit" (1 Corinthians 6:19) is common to most English translations; nothing else is quoted.

## Stage 2: Paper 1 Section 2

- [ ] **p1s2-purgatory-01**: 2 Maccabees 12:44-46 (praying for the dead) is in Catholic Bibles but not in most Protestant ones. Check CCC 1030-1032.
- [ ] **p1s2-hell-01**: CCC 1033-1037 (hell as self-exclusion; God predestines no one to hell).
- [ ] **p1s2-faith-works-01**: 'sola fide' is a simplified label for the Protestant position. Lutheran-Catholic dialogue (the 1999 Joint Declaration on the Doctrine of Justification) found much common ground. Check CCC 1987 and 2007-2011.
- [ ] **p1s2-abortion-01**: Jeremiah 1:5 is used as support for the sanctity of life before birth. Check CCC 2270-2275. The scene deliberately avoids mentioning any time limits or legal details.
- [ ] **p1s2-same-sex-respect-01**: CCC 2358 says people with same-sex attraction must be accepted with "respect, compassion and sensitivity". The feedback paraphrases this.
- [ ] **p1s2-same-sex-marriage-01**: the wrong-option note says the Methodist Church in Britain conducts same-sex weddings (it voted to allow them in 2021). Please check. The game avoids the 2023 document *Fiducia Supplicans* on blessings, because its interpretation is debated.
- [ ] **p1s2-separation-safety-01**: CCC 2383 (separation can be legitimate; civil divorce may be tolerated). The feedback directs the person to the police or a domestic abuse service. Check that this suits your school's safeguarding guidance.
- [ ] **p1s2-remarriage-01**: CCC 1650-1651 (the divorced and remarried are not separated from the Church). The question deliberately avoids receiving Communion.
- [ ] **p1s2-contraception-01**: Humanae Vitae (1968). The Anglican Lambeth Conference accepted contraception in some circumstances in 1930.
- [ ] **p1s2-ivf-01** and **p1s2-genetic-engineering-01**: CCC 2374-2379 and Dignitas Personae (2008), which allows somatic gene therapy and opposes enhancement and the destruction of embryos. Check that the simplified wording is acceptable.
- [ ] **p1s2-purposes-marriage-01**: at a Catholic wedding the couple are asked whether they will accept children lovingly from God. This is a paraphrase of the Rite of Marriage.
- [ ] Other references used: 1 Corinthians 15:42-44; Ephesians 2:8-10; James 2:14-17; Mark 1:15; Luke 17:20-21; John 18:36; Luke 23:39-43; Mark 10:6-12; Genesis 1:28; Exodus 20:12; Matthew 19:12; 1 Corinthians 7:32-35. The single word 'dead' from James 2:17 is the only quotation.
