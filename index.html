The Confessional

A revision game for Edexcel International GCSE Religious Studies: Christianity

How to play

You are a Catholic parish priest. People come to the confessional with a problem. Choose the advice that best matches Catholic teaching.

The other answers can sound kind or sensible. Often they are what other Christians, Humanists or atheists might say. A wrong answer snuffs out a candle. If all three candles go out, the bishop will come to see you!
